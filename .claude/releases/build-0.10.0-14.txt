Build 0.10.0 (14) - Copy/Paste Food Log Entries

What's New
- Copy/Paste meals: Copy all food entries from one day and paste them to another day using the new header control
- HealthKit weight import: Automatically sync weight data from Apple Health for improved tracking
- Dose projections: Enhanced projections on the GLP-1 analytics chart

How Copy/Paste Works
- In Food Log view, tap the copy/paste segmented control in the header
- Switch to Copy mode, tap on a day to copy its entries
- Switch to Paste mode, tap on a target day to paste
- Quick way to replicate similar meal days

Bug Fixes
- Fixed analytics refresh button not working
- Improved paste dialog positioning to anchor below header control

Focus Areas for Testing
