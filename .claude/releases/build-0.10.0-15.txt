Build 0.10.0 (15) - Food Scheduling

What's New
- Food Scheduling: Plan your meals ahead of time with a new scheduling system
- Schedule entry points: Access scheduling from the Food Log header and individual meal entries
- Schedule management: View, edit, and delete scheduled meals from a central management screen
- Meal planning: Create schedules that repeat daily, on specific weekdays, or on custom dates

How Scheduling Works
- In Food Log view, tap the calendar icon in the header to access Schedule Management
- Long-press on any food entry to schedule it for future dates
- Choose frequency: daily, specific days of the week, or one-time
- Scheduled meals appear automatically on their target dates
