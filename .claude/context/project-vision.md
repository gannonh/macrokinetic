# Project Vision

## Long-term Vision
JabTracker aims to become the definitive platform for GLP-1 medication management, transforming how patients understand and manage their treatment while providing healthcare providers with unprecedented insights into real-world medication adherence and effectiveness. The vision extends beyond simple tracking to become a comprehensive diabetes and weight management ecosystem.

## Strategic Objectives

### 5-Year Goals
1. **Market Leadership**: Become the #1 GLP-1 medication tracking app in the App Store
2. **Clinical Integration**: Standard tool recommended by endocrinologists and diabetes educators
3. **Platform Expansion**: Multi-platform presence (iOS, Android, Apple Watch, iPad)
4. **Healthcare Ecosystem**: Direct EMR integration and provider dashboard tools
5. **Research Contribution**: Anonymized data contributing to clinical research and outcomes

### 10-Year Vision
- **Comprehensive Health Platform**: Expand beyond GLP-1 to all injectable diabetes medications
- **AI-Powered Insights**: Machine learning for personalized treatment optimization
- **Global Reach**: International expansion with regulatory compliance worldwide
- **Clinical Trials Integration**: Platform for patient-reported outcomes in pharmaceutical research
- **Health System Partnerships**: Enterprise solutions for health systems and payers
