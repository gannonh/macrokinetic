Apple assigns ratings from questionnaire responses. Frequent medical/treatment information can produce a 16+ rating on current OS categories. Do not treat prescribed drug references as recreational drug-use content automatically, or assume the app is 4+. [Apple rating definitions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions), [Apple rating setup](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/set-an-app-age-rating/).

Apps in Medical or Health & Fitness distributed in the EU/EEA, UK, or US need a regulated-medical-device declaration. That requirement does not mean this app is a regulated device. The Account Holder or Admin records the supported answer. [Apple device declaration](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/declare-regulated-medical-device-status/).

Drug dosage calculators have separate entity or approval requirements. A disclaimer does not establish eligibility. The planned launch omits these calculators under KAT-3581. [Apple guideline 1.4.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
