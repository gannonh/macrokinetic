MacroKinetic is intended to record food, body measurements, and prescribed medication doses. Optional Apple Health integration, if enabled in the final release, requests supported measurements with your permission. You can decline Health access and use manual logging where available.

## Data and storage

The app holds profile information, food logs, body measurements, medication records, goals, and settings. Existing progress-photo records may remain in storage even while new photo capture is disabled.

MacroKinetic 1.0 keeps all of this data on your iPhone. It has no account or sign-in and no iCloud sync. The developer does not receive or have access to your data. If you back up your iPhone under your own Apple settings, the backup can include app data. The app's data-collection answer is "Data Not Collected"; that answer is PENDING OWNER APPROVAL and verification of the release build under KAT-3713 and KAT-3586.

## Choices and deletion

Deleting MacroKinetic from your iPhone removes its data. Apple Health records and the app's own copies are separate: deleting the app does not remove samples in Apple Health, which you manage in the Health app. Data export is UNRESOLVED. Confirm the deletion behavior on the release build under KAT-3586 before publication.
