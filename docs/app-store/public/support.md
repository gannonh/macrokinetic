# MacroKinetic support draft

**DRAFT. DO NOT PUBLISH. Final supported behavior is unverified on the release candidate. KAT-3783 publishes this page at https://macrokinetic.app/support.**

Support contact: support@macrokinetic.app.

For an app problem, describe what you tried, what happened, the app version/build, and your device/OS. Avoid emailing medication history, progress photos, or other sensitive records unless necessary.

## Help topics to verify

- Food search and logging work offline using the bundled database. Candidate verification is pending KAT-3587 and KAT-3590.
- Nutrition can be used without medication tracking. The confirmed first-run steps are pending KAT-3588.
- Apple Health access is optional if included in the release. The exact settings and permission-revocation steps need final device verification.
- MacroKinetic has no account and no sign-in. Your data stays on your iPhone and deleting the app removes it. Verification on the release build is pending KAT-3586 and KAT-3713.

MacroKinetic support cannot prescribe medication or answer urgent medical questions. Contact your healthcare professional for medical decisions and use local emergency services for emergencies.
