```markdown
## Day Status Handling

### Day Classification

Days are classified for calculation purposes:

| Day Type | Food Entries | Fasting Marked | Treatment |
|----------|--------------|----------------|-----------|
| Complete | Has entries  | No             | Include with logged calories |
| Fasting  | Any (usually 0) | Yes         | Include as 0-calorie day |
| Blank    | None         | No             | Skip (unknown data) |
| Partial (Today) | Any   | Any            | Exclude from calculations |

### Today Exclusion

Today is always excluded from energy balance and TDEE calculations because:
- The day is incomplete - including partial data skews averages downward
- Users may still be logging food
- MacroFactor research shows partial days can negatively impact expenditure calculations for ~3 weeks

Only completed days (yesterday and earlier) feed into TDEE calculations.

### Fasting Days

When a user marks a day as "Fasting":
- The day counts as a 0-calorie day in all calculations
- Food consistency score includes fasting days (they are intentional data points)
- Distinguishes intentional fasting from forgotten logging

### Blank Days

Days with no food entries AND not marked as fasting are skipped:
- Treated as "we don't know what happened"
- Not counted in food consistency denominator
- Does not contribute to averages
```
