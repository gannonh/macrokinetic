<objective>
Change the drug concentration graph from a line chart to a histogram (bar chart) format.

Purpose: A histogram better visualizes discrete concentration levels over time intervals, making it easier to see concentration patterns and how they relate to dose timing. Line charts imply continuous interpolation which isn't accurate for pharmacokinetic modeling with discrete data points.

Output: ConcentrationTimelineChart renders using BarMark instead of LineMark, with appropriate bar widths for the time intervals.
</objective>
