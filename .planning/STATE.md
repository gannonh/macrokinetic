## Project Summary

**Building:** Custom foods feature - users can create and manage personalized foods in their Food Library with modified macros, serving sizes, and barcode assignment.

**Core requirements:**
- "To Custom" button creates personalized version of any food
- Two save actions: "Create" and "Create & Add"
- Custom foods appear in dedicated "My Foods" section
- Custom foods prioritized in search results
- Barcode assignment via camera scan or manual entry

**Constraints:**
- CloudKit sync for cross-device access
- Offline-first functionality
- Follow existing MVVM architecture and SwiftData patterns
