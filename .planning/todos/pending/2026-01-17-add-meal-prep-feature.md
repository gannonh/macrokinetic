## Problem

Target users are bodybuilders and fitness enthusiasts who meal prep in bulk. Currently the app only supports logging individual meals as they're eaten. These users prepare meals ahead of time (often a week's worth) and need to:

1. Create meal prep batches (e.g., "5 containers of chicken, rice, and broccoli")
2. Easily log pre-prepped meals without re-entering all ingredients
3. Track macros for their prepared meal inventory
4. Quickly select from their prepped meals when logging

Without this feature, meal preppers must manually re-enter the same complex meals repeatedly, which is friction for our power users.
